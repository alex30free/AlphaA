How the indices are built
Sector index
Each sector is an equal-weight index of its stocks, starting at 100 in January 2006. Every week the index moves by the average weekly price change of its stocks, so a 2 kr stock counts as much as a 900 kr stock. Stocks listed later (for example Evolution, Epiroc, Sagax) join from their first full week without making the line jump.
Prices
Weekly closing prices (the last trading day of each week) from Yahoo Finance, adjusted by Yahoo for share splits and spin-offs. Dividends are not added back, so it is a price index, like a normal price chart.
MA40
The average of the last 40 weekly values, about 200 trading days. Above the MA40 = uptrend, below = downtrend. "Since" is the week the line last crossed its MA40.
Caveat
Moving-average signals lag and can flip back and forth in sideways markets. This is an overview tool, not a buy or sell signal.
